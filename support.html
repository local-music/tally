How can we help?

Need a hand with Tally? Get in touch and we'll do our best to help.

Email support
support@kook.love

When contacting support

To help us understand the issue, include:

  • A short description of what you were trying to do.
  • What happened and what you expected to happen.
  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem (please hide private or financial information).

Privacy and account questions

If your message is about privacy or information in the app, mention that in the subject line. Please do not email passwords or sensitive financial information.
