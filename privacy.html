Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tally ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the Tally app and this website.

Important: This is a starter privacy policy. Before submitting Tally to the App Store, make sure every statement matches the app's actual data collection, storage, analytics, advertising and third-party services.

Information you enter

Tally may let you enter business information such as revenue, expenses, costs, inventory, equipment, savings goals, tasks and wishlist items. Only enter information you are comfortable managing through the app.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the app's features, display your records and help you organise your business activity. We do not use information for purposes unrelated to providing and maintaining Tally unless we explain that separately and have an appropriate basis to do so.

Storage and sharing

The way information is stored and whether it is transmitted to or shared with service providers depends on the app's implemented features and configuration. We do not sell your personal information. If Tally uses cloud storage, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or other third-party services, this policy must be updated to identify those services and explain the information they process.

Your choices

You can choose what information you enter into Tally. You should also use any deletion, export or reset controls provided in the app. If you have a privacy question or request, contact us using the details on our Support page.

Children's privacy

Tally is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the situation.

Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the app's design to protect information. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Tally changes. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@kook.love.
